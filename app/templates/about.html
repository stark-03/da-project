How verification works

  1. Upload & preview. The image is checked (type, real content, size) and stored privately — only you can view it.
  2. Pre-processing. The image is resized, its perspective and tilt are corrected, and a denoised, contrast-enhanced copy is made for text extraction. The original is never changed.
  3. Text extraction (OCR). Tesseract OCR reads the text and the position of every word.
  4. Analysis. Independent checks measure:
    • text alignment — are all lines parallel and words on their baselines?
    • blurred or unclear regions — is any area much blurrier than the rest?
    • the photograph — is it sharp, and do its sharpness and noise match the document?
    • possible alterations — does any region have different image noise or JPEG compression history?
    • image quality — focus, exposure, contrast and OCR readability.
  5. Decision. A transparent rule-based engine combines the findings into a result.

What the results mean

Genuine
No significant indicators of alteration were found by these checks. It is not proof the document is authentic or belongs to the person presenting it.
Suspicious
Several or strong indicators of possible alteration were measured. Inspect the highlighted areas and the physical document; it does not prove fraud.
Inconclusive
The image quality was too poor, the text unreadable, or the evidence mixed. A safe decision cannot be made automatically — retake the photo or check manually.

Important limitations

  • No government or issuer database is checked.
  • The decision engine is rule-based. No machine-learning model has been trained yet because no labelled dataset of genuine and forged documents is available, so no accuracy figure exists.
  • The indicator score is a sum of rule weights, not a probability.
  • Skilled forgeries, or documents altered before printing, may show no visual indicators. Genuine documents photographed with glare, folds or stamps may show indicators.

Privacy

Documents are visible only to the account that uploaded them. Images and extracted text are deleted automatically after {{ config.RETENTION_DAYS }} days, and you can delete a document at any time.
